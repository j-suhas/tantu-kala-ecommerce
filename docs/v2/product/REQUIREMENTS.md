## 1. Purpose and context

Tantu Kala ("art made from threads") sells handmade crochet, macramé and pipe-cleaner jewelry, decor, gifting combos, festival
pieces and custom work. In Rakhi 2026 it sold 100+ units across ~20–30 orders (target: 500
rakhi units), and **most orders came through WhatsApp, Instagram or in person**, not the website.

v2 therefore has two jobs:

1. **Storefront:** a premium, mobile-first shop that converts Instagram/WhatsApp visitors.
2. **Single record of the business:** every order, whatever the channel, is logged, paid,
   tracked and measured in one place.

## 0. Requirement lifecycle and quality bar

Status flow: **Draft → Validated** (passes the `validate-requirement` checks; missing values
listed in `OWNER-INPUTS.md`) **→ Approved** (owner) **→ Implemented → Verified** (QA + the lead
validates intent). Only Approved requirements become Ready tasks.
