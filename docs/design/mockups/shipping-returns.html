Shipping & Returns

Everything at Tantu Kala is made by hand, to order — which shapes how shipping, returns and exchanges work here. Here's what to expect, in plain language.

Made to order

Shipping

Nothing on Tantu Kala sits pre-made on a shelf — each piece is crocheted or wire-worked after you order it. That's why our timelines are quoted in a range rather than "ships today."

How long before my order ships?

Most pieces are handmade and dispatched within roughly 3–6 business days of your order being confirmed and paid for. The exact estimate for a specific piece is shown on its product page — simpler items (a single keychain, a coaster) tend to be on the faster end; multi-piece combos and detailed jewelry can take a little longer.

This range is illustrative for this mockup and will be reviewed before launch.

Where do you ship to?

We currently ship across India via courier/India Post, to any serviceable pin code. We don't offer international shipping in v1 — if that changes, it'll be called out clearly on the site.

How will I know my order has shipped?

Once your piece is ready and dispatched, we'll share the courier and tracking details over WhatsApp and/or email — whichever you gave us at checkout.

Do timelines change around festivals?

During high-demand periods — Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Diwali — made-to-order lead times can run longer than the usual range, simply because more orders are being handworked at once. We'll flag this on the site when it applies, and will always try to communicate a realistic ship date rather than guess optimistically.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping charges (if any) are shown at checkout before you pay — this mockup doesn't fix a specific fee or a free-shipping threshold, since that's a pricing decision for the owner to confirm separately.

Standard products

Returns & Exchanges

Because each order is made specifically for you rather than pulled from ready stock, we lean on exchange or store credit for genuine issues, rather than an open-ended "return for any reason" policy.

My item arrived damaged, or I received the wrong item

Message us on WhatsApp or email within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and a photo/video of the item as received. Once verified, we'll arrange a free exchange or store credit — this is our priority resolution path for anything damaged in transit or shipped incorrectly.

Can I return an item just because I changed my mind?

Since every piece is handworked to order, we generally don't offer no-questions-asked returns for change-of-mind. If something about the piece genuinely doesn't work for you, reach out anyway — we'd rather hear from you and see what we can do than have you unhappy with a handmade piece.

Is slight colour or texture variation grounds for a return?

Handmade thread and pipe-cleaner pieces can have small, natural variation in colour, tension or finish from batch to batch — that's part of being handmade, not a defect, so minor variation on its own isn't treated as a return-eligible issue.

Do I get a cash refund?

For approved cases, our default is exchange or store credit rather than a cash refund, reflecting how small and made-to-order this operation is. Any exceptions would be handled case by case by the team.

Different rules apply

Custom Orders

Made to your brief

A custom order is built to your specific colours, size or reference — which means it generally can't be resold to anyone else if you change your mind. Because of that:

Custom orders are non-returnable and non-refundable once production has started. Before we begin, you're welcome to discuss and adjust the brief with us as much as you need.

The one exception: if a custom piece arrives damaged in transit, the same damaged-item process from the section above still applies — we'll make it right.

Direct UPI

Payment

We accept payment via Direct UPI — pay using the UPI button on mobile or by scanning the QR code on desktop, using your own UPI app.

Your order is recorded first; once you pay, our team manually verifies the payment against your order reference before your piece goes into production and ships. This manual check may take a little time — we'll follow up with you if anything doesn't match.

We don't yet support cards, netbanking or other gateways in this version of the site.
