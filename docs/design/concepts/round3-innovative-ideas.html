Round 3 — pushing further

More ambitious, still restrained: interactive ideas

Seven ideas this round lean more interactive than rounds 1–2 — a scroll-linked story, a small working gift-box builder, a custom cursor trail, even a mini pattern designer that feeds into Custom Orders. All still inside the existing palette, all still just demos here — nothing is wired into the real pages.

Idea 1 · Scroll-linked storytelling

"Unspool the thread" story reveal

A vertical thread line fills in as you scroll through a short brand story — each beat lights up as the thread reaches it. Could open the About page or a product's "making of" section.

One spool.Everything starts as plain crochet thread or a pipe cleaner.
One hook.Worked stitch by stitch, by hand, no machines involved.
One maker.Small batches, checked piece by piece before they ship.
One piece, for you.That's the whole process — nothing hidden, nothing automated.

Idea 3 · Signature micro-interaction

A thread-trail cursor, on hero sections

Moving the mouse across a hero banner leaves a soft, fading trail of thread-colored dots — a small, ownable signature touch, desktop-only, easy to keep subtle.

Idea 7 · Product delight

Gift-combo "untie the ribbon" reveal

A gifting-combo card shows a wrapped box by default; hovering (or tapping on mobile) untiles the ribbon away to reveal what's inside — a small unboxing moment before you've even bought it.
