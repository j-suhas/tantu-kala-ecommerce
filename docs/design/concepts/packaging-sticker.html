Logo: original vs vector redraw

Approved for pack printing only (owner, 21 Oct). Not the master logo: before master use, redraw the cords under the bands (they should bend into nested V shapes, as in the original).

Usage rule: use the simplified small mark only below about 25 mm tall, and the full mark above. On these packs: seal A, small mark at 15 mm; header B front, full mark at 26 mm; B back, small mark at 20 mm.

The repo only has logo.jpeg (1440 × 1440 px raster, peach #C58E77 on Umber #4D3126) and an 858 px app icon, and the owner has no vector file. So the mark and wordmark were traced by hand into docs/design/concepts/logo-vector-draft.svg: Umber, transparent background, in the original's own pixel coordinates so the two can be laid on top of each other. A simplified small-size mark is included for the 15 mm seal. The sticker artwork and the print files use the redraw.

How faithful the redraw is (my judgement; owner approved it for packs on 21 Oct)

  • Close: overall proportions and outline (hanger, dowel with brush ends, 12 knots, 23 cords, two chevron bands, V-shaped fringe), positions within a few pixels of the original, Umber colour. The wordmark is very close: same letter positions, round stroke ends, 10.5-unit strokes.
  • Different: (1) the knot bands are a regular chain of X-crosses; the original's crosses are irregular and denser. (2) In the original, the cords just above each band bend diagonally into nested V shapes; the redraw keeps them straight, ending in a stepped V. (3) The loops over the dowel are evenly spaced bars; the original's are irregular. (4) The redraw is centred on x = 702 px; the original's hanger sits about 3–4 px left of its cords. (5) Exact stroke widths and curve shapes are estimates from a 1440 px JPEG.
  • Simplified small mark: deliberately not faithful in detail. It has 12 cords instead of 23, 6 knots, a solid dowel and single ties in the bands, so it survives printing at 15 mm. Approved for pack printing (owner, 21 Oct).
  • Before any master-logo use: fix difference (2), the cords under the bands; ideally the original logo maker checks it.

Option A

Round seal, 40 mm Approved for print (owner, 21 Oct): ALL CAPS tagline

A round sticker that also closes the pack. Fold the bag's flap to the front and stick the seal centred over the flap edge. It sits in the top band of the pack, above the product. It works on every pack size we expect, from a small jewellery bag to a clear box lid (stick it across the lid edge). Content: the simplified mark, the wordmark, "ART MADE FROM THREADS" along the top and @tantu_kala_ along the bottom.

Pack rule: bag length ≥ product length + about 50 mm, so the seal (which reaches about 48 mm down from the top edge) never overlaps the product.

On a transparent pack

Placement and visibility

  • Where: front of the pack, centred on the flap edge, about 8 mm below the top. On a clear box, across the lid edge.
  • Covers: 1,257 mm², about 8% of a 100 × 150 mm bag front, 17% of a 75 × 100 mm bag and 4% of a 150 × 200 mm bag.
  • Product hidden: none, if the pack follows the rule bag length ≥ product length + about 50 mm.
  • Bonus: it doubles as a closure seal that holds the flap shut. It is not tamper-evident: a paper sticker can be peeled off and put back.
  • Size Ø 40 mm
  • Text 6.5–7 pt
  • Mark 15 mm
  • Inks 1–2

On each material, and the 1-colour version

White paper, full colour Ivory ground, Umber ink, Mustard ring. Umber on ivory 10.68:1.
White paper, 1-colour Umber Cheapest. Umber on white 11.78:1.
Kraft, 1-colour Umber 4.93:1 on mid kraft, 3.73:1 on dark kraft (see contrast). Needs its own proof: uncoated kraft spreads ink, so the mark's gaps may fill in.
Clear vinyl, no white ink Fails Readability depends on what shows behind: 11.78:1 over white, 1.15:1 over navy.
Clear vinyl + white flood Readable everywhere (11.78:1), but it looks like a white sticker. Needs a shop with white ink.
Why the seal isn't brown like the original logo. At 40 mm the mark is only 15 mm tall. Even the simplified mark's lines are about 0.15 mm, below the about 0.18 mm usually advised for light lines on a dark ground, which can fill in. So the seal uses dark ink on a light ground. The brown "logo look" is kept for the larger header tag (B).

Tagline on A: ALL CAPS approved (owner, 21 Oct)

"ART MADE FROM THREADS" (21 characters) runs along the top of the ring. The owner chose ALL CAPS. I had recommended sentence case (brand note S2: caps compete with the all-caps wordmark); it is kept below as "not chosen".

Fit (measured by eye on the Chrome render): both fit comfortably. Sentence case spans about ±55° from the top of the ring; caps about ±62°. Both stay well inside the roughly ±75° beyond which ring text starts to turn steeply down the sides, and well clear of the two dots at ±135°. All text sits inside the 36 mm safe circle. Re-check after the text is outlined: a different fallback font would change the length.

Assumptions and open questions

Assumptions (not facts)

  • Pack sizes shown (75 × 100, 100 × 150, 120 × 190, 150 × 200 mm) are illustrative.
  • Kraft colour #C4A27A (range #D2B48C–#B08A5E); real kraft varies by supplier.
  • Local print shops can die-cut a 40 mm circle and print CMYK on paper sticker stock; white ink is not assumed.
  • Minimum printable line about 0.09 mm dark-on-light and about 0.18 mm light-on-dark: general print practice, not a figure from a specific shop.
  • 300–350 gsm card for the header; Ø 6 mm hang hole.
  • Text widths and arc fit are judged by eye on Chrome's rendering of Karla; the outlined file must be re-checked.
  • Rough CMYK for the shop (simple conversion, not colour-managed; ask for a printed proof): Umber ≈ C0 M36 Y51 K70; Warm Ivory ≈ C0 M3 Y8 K2; Mustard ≈ C0 M23 Y66 K21; logo peach ≈ C0 M28 Y40 K23.
