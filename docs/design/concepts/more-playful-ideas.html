More playful ideas — brainstorm round 2

Fun facts, a lite animated story, and a few more engagement ideas

A fresh batch, separate from the six already wired into the approved pages. Nothing here is implemented anywhere yet — this is a sampler for you to react to. Hover/scroll each demo below.

A small four-step animated line-drawing strip — thread, stitch, shape, finished piece — looping gently on the About or a product page. Quiet motion, not a full video.

Idea 2 · Fun facts

A scrolling fun-facts ribbon

A slow auto-scrolling strip of bite-sized handmade facts — could sit in the footer or between homepage sections, always moving gently in the background.

Idea 3 · Authenticity touch

A small maker's mark on product pages

A quiet "Piece No. 048 of this design" style stamp near the product title — reinforces handmade-not-mass-produced without shouting about it.

TK Piece No. 048 of this design

Idea 4 · Fun facts + storytelling

Thread-colour name stories

Hovering a colour swatch on a product page reveals a one-line story behind that shade's name — small, optional delight for someone who lingers.

Idea 6 · Warmth / social proof

A corkboard of tiny thank-you notes

Idea 7 · Playful education

"Spot the handmade variation"

Two nearly-identical thumbnails with a hover reveal pointing out a tiny natural difference — reinforces the Shipping & Returns note that small variation is a handmade trait, not a flaw, in a lighter way than a policy paragraph.
